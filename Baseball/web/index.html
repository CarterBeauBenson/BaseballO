Who has the highest Empty Game Rate?

Empty games and offensive games played for each batter.

What counts as an Empty Game?

A player appeared as a batter but recorded no reviewed qualifying offensive contribution in that game.

  • Single, double, triple, or home run
  • Walk
  • Sacrifice fly
  • Fielder’s choice
  • Explicitly adjudicated stolen base

Damage Score for one failed plate appearance

min(100, 100 × base pressure / 6 × out pressure × quick-failure pressure × double-play pressure)

  • Base pressure = 1 for first + 2 for second + 3 for third.
  • Out pressure = (outs before the plate appearance + 1) ÷ 3.
  • Grind Score = pitches + swings + 2 × contacts + 2 × fouls.
  • Quick-failure pressure = the smaller of 1 or 6 ÷ Grind Score (with a minimum denominator of 1).
  • Double-play pressure = 1.25 for a double play; otherwise 1.
  • Game Damage Score = the sum of its failed plate-appearance Damage Scores.

Plate Appearance Quality · PAQ-1.0 A baseball-style .000–1.000 rating combining authoritative outcome, bounded grind, and situational evidence. Excellent .850+Good .650+Mixed .450+Poor .250+Bad below .250
Show math

PAQ-1.0 = 80% outcome + 15% grind + 5% situation, rounded to three decimals.

Outcome ratings: home run 1.000; triple .950; double .850; walk .800; hit by pitch .750; single .700; catcher interference or sacrifice fly .650; sacrifice bunt or field error .550; fielder’s choice .450; ordinary field/force out .150; strikeout .050; double or triple play .000; any other mapped result .300.

Grind rating: min(1.000, (pitches + swings + 2 × contacts + 2 × fouls) ÷ 18).

Situation rating: .000 for a double or triple play; .850 when another runner advances safely; .250 when a runner is put out; otherwise .500.

Damage cap: grounded-into-double-play and triple-play plate appearances cannot score above .249.

Current evidence boundary: mapped contact contributes through Grind. PAQ-1.0 does not yet include Statcast batted-ball measurements.
