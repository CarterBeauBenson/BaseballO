# Decisions needed

| ID | Question | Proposed conservative answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NUMBER-01 | What does a uniform-number Code Identifier designate? | A team-scoped Player Role, never the Person intrinsically. |
| NUMBER-02 | Does 'primaryNumber' prove a Uniform Number Assignment Act? | No. A bare snapshot does not identify the declarative Act, Team agent, or its output event. |
| NUMBER-03 | Can the Code Identifier be asserted without the Act? | Decide whether a response-versioned information assertion may designate an independently established Player Role, or whether all number assertions require assignment evidence. |
| NUMBER-04 | What establishes team and interval scope? | Require accepted team-scoped role/stint identity plus effective bounds; 'currentTeam' and acquisition time alone must not become an invented historical interval. |
| NUMBER-05 | How are reused numbers handled? | Different Player Roles may be designated by lexically equal Code Identifiers in different team/stint contexts; never merge by number text alone. |

NUMBER-01 and NUMBER-02 reuse accepted semantics. NUMBER-03 and NUMBER-04 are
the remaining source admission decisions.
