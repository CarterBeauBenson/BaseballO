# Competency questions

| ID | Question | Candidate answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PT-01 | What identifies one Player Role? | One Person, one Baseball Team organizational context, and one uninterrupted stint. A return after Loss creates a new Role. |
| PT-02 | What persists? | The Person and exact Role participate in one Stasis of Role occupying one continuous Temporal Interval. |
| PT-03 | What begins and ends it? | Supported Gain and Loss Processes affect the exact Role, have the Person as participant, occupy their own regions, and respectively precede/follow the Stasis. |
| PT-04 | What realizes the Role? | Supported Player Acts, not the Stasis, source observation, Date Identifier, Gain, or Loss. |
| PT-05 | What happens with incomplete history? | Left-censored histories omit invented Gain; open histories omit invented Loss; missing observations never terminate a Role. |
| PT-06 | Where is history integrated? | Source RML remains separate. Cross-source reconciliation occurs only after promotion in an explicitly scoped derived product. |

## Negative tests

- No one-Role-per-game or one-Role-per-source-row minting.
- No timeless Person-to-Team shortcut.
- No Stasis realization edge.
- No Gain/Loss without exact Role identity and direction evidence.
