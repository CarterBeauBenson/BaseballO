# Competency questions

1. What Fiat Lines are relata of an Angle Quality?
2. At which shared Fiat Point do those Fiat Lines intersect?
3. What material entities, sites, or process histories determine those Fiat
   Lines at the relevant time?
4. Which Measurement ICE is a measurement of the Angle Quality, and which
   angular Measurement Unit and Reference System does it use?
5. What two nonidentical Fiat Points are relata of a Distance Quality?
6. Which material entities or sites determine those Fiat Points at the
   relevant time?
7. Which Measurement ICE is a measurement of the Distance Quality, and which
   linear Measurement Unit and Reference System does it use?
8. Can the same generic geometry support a venue field dimension, an arm
   angle, and a bat-path angle without creating a provider-field class?
9. Can an angle or distance exist without any Statcast or MLB record describing
   it?

Negative tests:

- a Measurement ICE is not an Angle Quality or Distance Quality;
- one isolated Fiat Line does not supply an Angle Quality;
- one isolated Fiat Point does not supply a Distance Quality;
- a signed X/Y displacement is not asserted as a Distance Quality merely
  because a source column uses the word "distance."
