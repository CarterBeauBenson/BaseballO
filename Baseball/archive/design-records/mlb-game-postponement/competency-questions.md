# Competency questions

1. Do original and revised schedule rows with the same stable MLB `gamePk`
   identify one Baseball Game?
2. Which Act changes the institutionally planned time of that Game?
3. Which Plan was in force before the Act, and which revised Plan was its
   output?
4. Which planned Temporal Interval did each Plan identify?
5. Which Agent issued the official postponement declaration?
6. What reason did MLB classify the postponement under without asserting that
   the classified reason is already a world-side cause?
7. Which plate appearances, pitches, and player performances are occurrent
   parts of the one Game associated with the postponement?
8. Can a query distinguish a pre-game postponement from a delay, suspension,
   resumption, or cancellation without treating those labels as synonyms?

## Reviewed answers recorded in conversation

- Stable `gamePk` identity is one Baseball Game across its schedule revisions.
- MLB is the Agent of the official declarative communication represented by its
  official schedule publication. The person or body that made any underlying
  operational decision remains unresolved unless separately evidenced.
- A reason such as `Inclement Weather` remains MLB nominal-classification
  evidence. It does not yet license an atmospheric Process, Quality, or causal
  relation. A future weather source may supply that world-side evidence.
