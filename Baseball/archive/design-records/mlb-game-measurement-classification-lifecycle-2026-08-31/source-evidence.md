# Source evidence

The ontologist accepted these decisions in conversation on 2026-08-31 after
review of the active MLB-game deferred-measurements package.

Official Baseball Savant CSV documentation identifies release speed as an
out-of-hand scalar in the Statcast era, places initial velocity and acceleration
components at `y=50` feet, defines plate coordinates from the catcher's
perspective, and distinguishes the front-of-plate evaluation through 2025 from
the middle-of-plate ABS evaluation beginning in 2026. Official MLB glossary
material defines exit velocity at contact and documents the provider's pitch
type taxonomy while allowing physical variation within its categories.

The public documentation does not define every similarly named `feed/live`
field. The accepted equivalence rule therefore requires same-event empirical
agreement plus compatible official semantics before such a field is mapped.

Official MLB offseason calendars distinguish the post-World-Series quiet
period, open free agency, tender and qualifying-offer events, Winter Meetings,
the Rule 5 Draft, arbitration exchanges, and international signing periods.
This evidence supports separating temporally extended periods from the Acts and
other Processes that occur during them.

No new object property is introduced by this decision.
