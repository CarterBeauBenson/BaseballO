# Accepted competency-question answers

1. An undocumented MLB Games field may acquire an executable meaning only
   after same-event evidence demonstrates agreement with an official MLB
   definition in value, unit, evaluation scope, frame, and method era. Name
   similarity alone is insufficient; contradictory or incomplete evidence
   leaves the field unresolved.
2. Changing motion is represented through evaluation-local Process Profiles.
   Release, plate-crossing, and immediate-post-contact evaluations are distinct
   occurrent parts of the relevant Ball Motion Process rather than scalar
   qualities of the whole motion.
3. A scalar magnitude is a Speed Process Profile. Signed components jointly
   support a Velocity Process Profile only when their common frame, axes,
   signs, unit, and evaluation scope are represented. Acceleration components
   follow the corresponding Acceleration Process Profile pattern.
4. Provider pitch and batted-ball labels are reusable Nominal Measurement ICEs
   under versioned Reference Systems. They classify particular real-world Acts;
   they do not replace those Acts or encode a universal grip, hold, trajectory,
   or motion shape.
5. Offseason is a Process with reviewed temporal parts for the free-agent quiet
   period, open free-agency period, salary-arbitration period, and international
   signing period. Qualifying offers, tenders, meetings, drafts, and exchanges
   are occurrents within the relevant periods, not season phases merely because
   they occur there.
6. Unresolved source individuals receive evidence-led entity-resolution work.
   A provider placeholder never licenses invention of a Person, Act, or
   physical Process; genuinely unresolved evidence remains unresolved or is
   quarantined under the source contract.
