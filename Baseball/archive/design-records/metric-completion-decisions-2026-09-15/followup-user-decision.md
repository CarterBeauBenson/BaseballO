# Hit-and-run responsibility and Contribution Path Diversity qualification

Accepted by Carter Beau Benson on 2026-09-15, before implementation.

The follow-up asked who bears the runner-out damage for an explicitly confirmed
called hit-and-run ending in a swinging strikeout and the runner thrown out,
and whether Contribution Path Diversity qualifies through either the batting
minimum or the independent-running minimum.

The user's exact answer was:

> Batter. Hit amd run failure is on the batter
> Either

For that confirmed failed hit-and-run, attribute the runner-out consequence
to the batter, alongside the batter's own out. Apply the existing destruction
and erosion formulas to those attributed outs. Do not also charge the same
consequence to the independent-running channel. Retain the actual runner,
out, and final states in the evidence. This is analytical responsibility;
it does not make the batter the agent of the runner's running act.

A swinging strikeout and caught stealing alone do not confirm a called
hit-and-run. This answer does not accept the earlier proposed equal split for
genuinely independent simultaneous outs or supply a source admission rule for
confirming a called strategy.

Contribution Path Diversity qualifies when either the existing batting
minimum or the existing independent-running minimum is met for the complete
selected range. With G applicable team games, these are nearest whole
3.1 * G PA (half upward), or max(3, ceil(G / 10)) independent running episodes.
A player with zero PA can qualify through running. Count distinct eligible
independent running episodes separately from positive contribution channels;
the entropy denominator is not the participation denominator. Neither minimum
is relaxed to fill five rows. The settled period-wide entropy formula stays
unchanged, with exact channel counts and approximate logarithmic evaluation.

These decisions introduce no ontology terms, object properties, identity
changes, source admission shortcuts, or semantic-freeze ratification.
