These answers supplement the separately published narrow RML repair approval.
They are recorded and published before their executable consequences.

1. **Targeted source reacquisition:** NiFi may fetch the existing MLB game
   endpoint for specifically identified games on a repair manifest when the
   needed retained inputs are unavailable. Start with K1's five identified
   games. Preserve distinct response hashes, reconcile existing identities,
   and map only the affected facts and dependencies. No season reacquisition,
   full-game mapping replacement or corpus rebuild.
2. **Overlapping temporal regions:** overlap is not itself a contradiction
   or proof that two distinct plays have an ambiguous game-state boundary.
   Fully reconciled play identities, runner transitions, outs, substitutions,
   operative corrections and boundary observations may independently support
   analytical before/after state. Numeric endpoints need not be artificially
   separated. Retain observed clock values and their uncertainty; invent no
   precision, tolerance, adjusted timestamp, strict precedence or new relation.
   Array position alone remains insufficient. Withhold an unresolved state
   transition, a genuinely required unknown time, or an inconsistent interval
   whose own end precedes its start. Existing T1 treatment of the latter stays
   in force. This clarifies evidence at accepted C1/C2 boundaries, not a new
   ontology of fuzzy time or a change to existing entity identities.
3. **Hit-and-run confirmation:** an explicit final MLB description identifying
   the called hit-and-run, reconciled with its actual participants and events,
   is sufficient evidence for the accepted batter-responsibility policy.
   Swinging strikeout plus caught stealing alone is insufficient. Do not
   invent a strategy Act or infer either channel's responsibility from silence.
4. **Independent shared-out erosion:** within a supported shared play,
   allocate surviving/stranded-runner erosion once, in proportion to each
   channel's distinct attributed outs. Preserve each out participant's own
   destruction cost. In the accepted first-and-third, one-out K/independent-CS
   example, batter damage is 3/4 and runner damage is 5/6. Do not combine
   separate sequential plays. Confirmed failed hit-and-runs remain entirely
   batter responsibility, without duplicate running damage.

The narrow RML approval together with answer 1 covers the existing-vocabulary
K1 compound double-play correction and its named source acquisition. The K1
package is archived with that disposition. No new ontology term, object
property or lifecycle topology is accepted. Ordinary source-owned context,
RML, SHACL, admission, query/index and SQL implementation is authorized within
these decisions; unrelated failures and incomplete populations remain visible.
