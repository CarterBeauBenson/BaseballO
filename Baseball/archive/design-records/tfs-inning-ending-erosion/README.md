# Accepted inning-ending opportunity erosion

The user selected **Charge stranded-runner erosion** on 2026-09-08.
[decision.json](decision.json) preserves the exact question, answer and scope.

With a runner on third and two outs, a batter strikeout incurs `1/4` batter
destruction and `1` runner erosion, giving TFS `-5/4 = -1.25`. The stranded
runner is not counted as another out or assigned destruction as well.
Positive evidence and completeness are still required to identify that runner.
