# Major League Free Agent Role

Status: **accepted 2026-08-30 - source-independent design**

This package proposes one world-side class, `MajorLeagueFreeAgentRole`, for
the positive institutional status borne by a Person whom the Major League
Baseball free-agency rules permit to negotiate and form a player contract with
eligible Baseball Teams. It is not the absence of a team-context Player Role.
An ordinary Person who has no contract with a Baseball Team does not thereby
bear this Role.

The Role is externally grounded by an applicable Baseball Rule that is also an
Action Permission. The Role may be realized in an Act of Contract Formation
permitted by that Rule. The Person and exact Role participate in one Stasis of
Role while the status persists. The Stasis is not an official MLB maintenance
act and does not realize the Role.

Different evidenced routes may precede the Gain of Role, including automatic
eligibility at a rule-specified boundary, release or non-tender consequences,
and a confirmed election of free agency. A later contract formation or
retirement may precede the Loss of the exact Role. Provider labels do not by
themselves determine which route occurred.

This accepted design introduces no object property and authorizes the named
ontology class. RML, SHACL, RDF, SQL, NiFi, and UI implementation still require
the source-specific Mermaid gate before any transaction mapping uses the class.
