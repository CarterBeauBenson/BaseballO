# Competency questions

| ID | Question | Candidate answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FA-ROLE-01 | Why is an unsigned former MLB player a free agent while an ordinary unsigned Person is not? | The former Person bears a positively grounded Major League Free Agent Role under an applicable Baseball Rule; absence of a team-context Player Role is never sufficient. |
| FA-ROLE-02 | What can realize the Role? | An Act of Contract Formation with a Baseball Team that is permitted by the applicable Baseball Rule may realize it. A Stasis of Role never realizes it. |
| FA-ROLE-03 | What keeps the status present through time? | The Person and exact Role participate in a Stasis of Role occupying the evidenced Temporal Interval. This is ontological persistence, not a continuously repeated MLB administrative act. |
| FA-ROLE-04 | How can the Role begin? | An evidenced Gain of Role may follow an automatic rule-defined boundary, release or non-tender consequence, or confirmed free-agency election. The route remains distinct from the Gain. |
| FA-ROLE-05 | How can the Role end? | An evidenced Loss of Role may follow contract formation or retirement. Signing can also precede Gain of a new team-context Player Role. |
| FA-ROLE-06 | May `Declared Free Agency` alone identify a declaration act or Gain? | No. The exact code and structured evidence must distinguish an automatic status change, player election, club act, or information-only report. |
| FA-ROLE-07 | Does this Role cover amateur, international, or Minor League free agency? | No. Those regimes require separate evidence and review; a generic provider use of "free agent" cannot be promoted to this class. |
| FA-ROLE-08 | How is an open or incomplete history handled? | Assert the Role and Stasis only from affirmative evidence. Missing earlier or later transactions do not license invented Gain or Loss processes. |

## Negative tests

- No Major League Free Agent Role is inferred from the absence of an active
  Player Role, roster row, contract row, or team identifier.
- A Stasis of Role never realizes a Major League Free Agent Role.
- A source record ICE, transaction-type measurement ICE, or provider label is
  never the Role, Gain, Stasis, Loss, or contract-formation Act.
- Signing as a free agent does not prove the source contains the complete
  beginning boundary of that Role.
- Major League, Minor League, amateur, international, posted-player, and other
  free-agency regimes are not silently identified.
