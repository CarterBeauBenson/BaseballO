# Source evidence

The MLB transaction code list contains Released, Declared Free Agency,
Retired, Assigned, Recalled, Optioned, Outrighted, Selected, Designated for
Assignment, Suspension, Waiver, Status Change, Acquired, and Obtained labels.
The labels are source classifications and do not by themselves identify their
referents.

The accepted world vocabulary supplies Act of Declarative Communication,
Directive Information Content Entity, Baseball Team, Player Role, Gain of
Role, Loss of Role, `has agent`, `has participant`, `has output`, `affects`,
and `precedes`. The separately reviewed Major League Free Agent Role supplies
the positive status target for supported free-agency routes. No new object
property is required.
