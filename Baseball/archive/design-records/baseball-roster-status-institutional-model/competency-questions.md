# Competency questions

| ID | Question | Candidate answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ROSTER-ACT-01 | What makes a roster-status report evidence for a world Act? | Structured evidence must establish the Act's Agent, Person participant, output Directive ICE, and independently grounded Role consequence. |
| ROSTER-ACT-02 | What grounds a Baseball Player Release Act? | A Baseball Team is agent; the Act outputs a release decision and precedes Loss of the exact team Player Role. |
| ROSTER-ACT-03 | Does release always create free agency? | No. Gain of Major League Free Agent Role requires separate rule and status evidence. |
| ROSTER-ACT-04 | What grounds a Free Agency Declaration Act? | A Person is agent in a declaration/election communicated through the league system, and the Act precedes Gain of the exact Major League Free Agent Role. Automatic free agency is not this Act. |
| ROSTER-ACT-05 | What grounds a Retirement Declaration Act? | The Person is agent, the Act has a retirement-decision output, and it precedes Loss of the exact active team Player Role or Major League Free Agent Role supported by evidence. |
| ROSTER-ACT-06 | Are provider codes enough for other specific Act subclasses? | No. The first RML pass retains information classification until the world-side differentia and consequences are established. |

## Negative tests

- A source record ICE is not an institutional Act or its Directive output.
- An automatic rule consequence is not a declaration Act.
- Release does not automatically entail free agency.
- Retirement does not end every baseball-related Role borne across a career.
- A provider code does not license a field-shaped Act or status-Role class.
