# Consolidated competency-question disposition

The accepted competency-question answers are the exact answers preserved in
the nineteen design records linked from
[`../FINAL-MLB-RML-REVIEW.md`](../FINAL-MLB-RML-REVIEW.md). They establish:

- source-neutral Day identity and temporal precision;
- persistent Role identity across Gain, Stasis, and Loss processes;
- grounded release, free-agency declaration, retirement, trade, and signing
  events while ungrounded transaction codes remain information-only;
- real batting, throwing, and fielding dispositions and information-level
  position descriptions;
- Person-borne mass measured in pounds; and
- persistent Venues with distinct reference points, material parts, Artifact
  Functions, Sites, and accommodation Amounts.

No answer authorizes Statcast or executable source mappings.
