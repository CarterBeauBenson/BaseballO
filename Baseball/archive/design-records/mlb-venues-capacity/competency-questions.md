# Competency questions

| ID | Question | Candidate answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CAPACITY-01 | What does the number measure? | A Spectator Accommodation Amount Quality inhering in the Baseball Venue and grounded in installed seats plus designed standing-room accommodation. |
| CAPACITY-02 | What are seats? | Spectator Seats are Material Artifacts that are continuant parts of the Venue and bear Sitting Artifact Functions. |
| CAPACITY-03 | What is standing room? | A Standing-Room Spectator Site is a Site that is a continuant part of the Venue. The Venue or its material infrastructure bears the accommodation Function; the Site does not. |
| CAPACITY-04 | Must spectators be present? | No. The Amount Quality and Artifact Functions may exist while no spectator-use Process occurs. Function existence is distinct from realization. |
| CAPACITY-05 | What is the temporal scope? | The response versions the measurement evidence. No configuration interval is invented; later evidence may establish stasis or change. |
| CAPACITY-06 | Does the endpoint evidence a counting Process? | No. Emit the world Quality and Measurement ICE without an invented agent, method, instrument, or counting event. |

## Negative tests

- Capacity is not current attendance.
- A Standing-Room Spectator Site does not bear an Artifact Function.
- The numeric total does not mint one Seat or standing place per unit.
- Retrieval time does not establish a configuration boundary.
- An empty Venue does not lose its Amount Quality or unrealized Functions.
