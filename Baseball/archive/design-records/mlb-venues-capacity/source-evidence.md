# Source evidence

The accepted venue reviews observed `fieldInfo.capacity` in both the official
venue response and the MLB game payload. The ontologist established that the
reported total measures installed seats and, where applicable, standing-room
space, both belonging to the stadium configuration.

CCO supplies Material Artifact, Structural Support Artifact Function, Amount,
Measurement ICE, and the required inherence and realization relations. BFO
supplies Site and continuant parthood. The selected model distinguishes the
Venue's persistent Amount Quality, the designed Functions, and any later
spectator-use Processes.

The source does not enumerate particular seats or standing places and does not
identify a counting, installation, or reconfiguration Process. No raw response
was acquired or retained for this proposal.
