# Accepted competency-question answers

1. BaseballO field-coordinate modeling must not instantiate BFO/CCO Spatial
   Region, Coordinate System Axis, x-Axis, or y-Axis classes.
2. The world-side field frame uses two Fiat Lines meeting at a shared origin
   Fiat Point. Its perpendicularity is an Angle Quality inhering in both Fiat
   Lines.
3. A coordinate-component magnitude is grounded in a Distance Quality between
   the origin Fiat Point and a selected Fiat Point on the corresponding axis
   Fiat Line. Direction and sign remain information interpreted under the
   accepted field-coordinate convention.
4. The coordinate tuple is a Designative Information Content Entity that is
   about the reviewed component qualities, uses a Baseball Field Coordinate
   Reference System ICE, and designates a field-relative Site.
5. The Baseball Field Coordinate Reference System ICE is a generic CCO
   Reference System, not a CCO Spatial Reference System.
6. Jena polygon containment may compare a point and polygon expressed under
   the same provider convention. It does not establish feet, latitude/
   longitude, physical distance, or a transformation to ballpark geometry.
7. No provider-coordinate mapping is executable until the source-specific
   origin, orientation, sign, scale, projection, polygon, and missing-value
   contract is reviewed.
