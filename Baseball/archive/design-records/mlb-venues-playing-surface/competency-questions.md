# Competency questions

| ID | Question | Candidate answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SURFACE-01 | What does `turfType` classify? | A Baseball Playing Surface, which is a real Material Artifact and continuant part of the Venue. |
| SURFACE-02 | Is the surface the Baseball Field Site? | No. The material surface and immaterial Site remain distinct. |
| SURFACE-03 | Why can the surface support play? | It bears a Structural Support Artifact Function grounded in its design. That Function may exist while unrealized. |
| SURFACE-04 | What do the provider values do? | They nominally classify the surface under a pinned Reference System; they do not create one ontology class per string. |
| SURFACE-05 | How is replacement handled? | A replaced material surface is a new particular. Response-scoped witnesses and evidence are retained without asserting unsupported same-as, different-from, or validity boundaries. |

## Negative tests

- A Baseball Field Site is not typed as grass, dirt, or artificial turf.
- The classification ICE is not the material surface.
- Retrieval season does not establish a season-long surface Stasis.
- No installation, replacement, or support Process is invented.
