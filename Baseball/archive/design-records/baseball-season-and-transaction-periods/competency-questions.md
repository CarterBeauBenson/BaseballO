# Competency questions

1. Can a season contain institutionally delimited Processes that contain no
   Games? **Proposed: yes**, through Baseball Season Segment.
2. Does Baseball Season Phase retain its existing game-containing condition?
   **Proposed: yes**, and it becomes a subclass of Baseball Season Segment.
3. Which game-containing classes are proposed? **Preseason Phase, Regular
   Season Phase, Postseason Phase, and All-Star Phase.**
4. Is Offseason a Season Phase? **Proposed: no.** It is a Baseball Season
   Segment because it need not contain a Game.
5. Is the Major League Free-Agent Quiet Period an occurrent part of an
   Offseason Segment? **Proposed: no.** It is a Temporal Interval that is
   interval contained by the Temporal Interval occupied by the Offseason
   Segment Process.
6. Are Open Free Agency, Salary Arbitration, and the International Signing
   Period necessarily contained wholly within Offseason? **Proposed: no.**
   Their official extents can overlap preseason or the playing season.
7. Are meetings, qualifying offers, tender acts, drafts, and exchanges
   themselves Season Segments? **Proposed: no.** They are occurrents whose own
   occupied Temporal Intervals can be interval contained by or overlap the
   applicable transaction-calendar Temporal Interval.
8. Can provider calendar labels trigger the reviewed subtype without replacing
   the entity with an ICE? **Proposed: yes**, through reusable Nominal
   Measurement ICEs under a versioned Reference System. Season types classify
   Processes; transaction-period types classify Temporal Intervals.
