# Competency questions

| ID | Question | Candidate answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TRADE-01 | Which rows form one Trade Act? | Rows sharing the accepted transaction-group identity are grouped only after code, direction, and party validation. |
| TRADE-02 | Who are the Agents? | Origin and destination Teams when structured evidence establishes their causal participation. |
| TRADE-03 | What are the Role consequences? | The Trade has Loss of old Player Role and Gain of new Player Role as occurrent parts; Loss precedes Gain. |
| TRADE-04 | What is the temporal precision? | Each transition occupies its own region, temporal part of the designated canonical Day when Day-level evidence is present. |

## Negative tests

- Mentioned Teams are not automatically Agents.
- Repeated IDs do not merge rows that fail the accepted grouping contract.
- Loss and Gain never affect an unidentified or source-row-shaped Role.
