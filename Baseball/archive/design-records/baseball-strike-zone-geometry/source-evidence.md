# Source evidence

The official rulebook concept supplies upper, lower, side-to-side, front, and
back boundaries over Home Plate. MLB's 2026 ABS description specifies a
two-dimensional evaluation plane at the midpoint of the 17-inch-wide plate,
with batter-relative top and bottom calculations. That evaluation plane does
not replace the three-dimensional rulebook strike-zone Site.

- <https://www.mlb.com/news/ball-strike-challenge-system-2026>
- <https://baseballsavant.mlb.com/csv-docs>

The Savant documentation also records an era boundary: plate coordinates use
the front of Home Plate through 2025 and its middle beginning in 2026 for ABS.
The same documentation does not, by name alone, prove equivalent semantics for
every MLB Games field. The accepted same-event evidence gate still applies.
