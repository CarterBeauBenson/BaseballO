# Competency questions

1. Is the rulebook strike zone a Site rather than an ICE or Spatial Region?
   **Proposed: yes.**
2. Is it three-dimensional? **Proposed: yes.** It has at least six Fiat Surface
   continuant parts: upper, lower, first-base side, third-base side, front, and
   back boundaries.
3. Is the 2026 ABS evaluation plane identical with the whole Strike Zone Site?
   **Proposed: no.** It is a Fiat Surface that is a continuant part of the Site.
4. What anchors the four vertical boundaries? **Proposed:** Fiat Lines selected
   from the relevant Home Plate boundaries under the applicable Strike Rule.
5. What anchors the upper and lower boundaries? **Proposed:** batter-relative
   anatomical Fiat Points selected under the applicable Strike Rule at the
   reviewed pitch evaluation.
6. Does the ontology derive those anchors from undocumented Games fields?
   **Proposed: no.** Source execution requires method- and era-specific evidence.
7. May an ABS Rule designate the fiat evaluation surface that its institutional
   specification creates? **Proposed: yes.** The surface remains a world-side
   fiat boundary; the Rule is information about and prescriptive of its use.
8. Does a two-dimensional rendering become the surface it depicts?
   **Proposed: no.**
9. Are the exact batter and Home Plate anchors complete enough for a
   necessary-and-sufficient Strike Zone Site axiom? **Proposed: no.** Retain
   necessary restrictions only until the dotted geometry is resolved.
