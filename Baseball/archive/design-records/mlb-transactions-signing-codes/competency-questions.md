# Competency questions

| ID | Question | Candidate answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SIGN-01 | What world Act does supported signing evidence identify? | An Act of Contract Formation with Person and Baseball Team as Agents. |
| SIGN-02 | What team Role consequence follows? | The contract Act precedes Gain of the exact new team-context Player Role. |
| SIGN-03 | When is the free-agent branch present? | Only with affirmative Major League Free Agent Role evidence; the contract Act may realize that Role and precedes its Loss. |
| SIGN-04 | Does signing prove an Act of Employment? | No additional Act of Employment is inferred without evidence at that grain. |

## Negative tests

- Absence of an old team Role never establishes free agency.
- A signing label without validated parties does not create a contract Act.
- Minor, amateur, international, and Major League free-agent regimes are not conflated.
