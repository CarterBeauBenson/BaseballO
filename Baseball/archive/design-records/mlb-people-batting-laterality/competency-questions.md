# Competency questions

| ID | Question | Candidate answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BAT-LAT-01 | What does the person-level code classify? | A real Batting Side Disposition inhering in the Person. The code measurement describes that disposition. |
| BAT-LAT-02 | What realizes the disposition? | Batter Acts in which the Person bats from the corresponding side. Stasis and source observation do not realize it. |
| BAT-LAT-03 | What does switch hitting mean? | The Person bears both a left-side and a right-side particular Batting Side Disposition. `S` is expanded into two classified dispositions, not a third-side disposition. |
| BAT-LAT-04 | Does the people assertion determine every Plate Appearance? | No. Event evidence may show which disposition was realized in a particular Plate Appearance and may expose exceptions or changes. |
| BAT-LAT-05 | How is history handled? | The source supports the disposition at observation time but not an invented beginning or ending boundary. Response-version the measurement evidence. |

## Negative tests

- A Person is not typed as left, right, or switch.
- A Nominal Measurement ICE is not the disposition it describes.
- The people record does not create an unobserved Batter Act.
- A switch hitter does not bear a fictional third-side disposition.
- An observed Plate Appearance does not overwrite the persistent source claim.
