# Source evidence

The implementation is bounded by the accepted MLB Games `feed/live` contract and the reviewed repository evidence already archived on 2026-08-31. The accepted decisions establish:

- nominal pitch and trajectory classifications are evidence about a current classification, not timeless necessary-and-sufficient class axioms;
- event-record identifiers are Identifier ICEs rather than routing literals;
- season phases are parts of the Baseball Season process;
- coordinates remain information-space evidence until provider-frame grounding is established;
- bunt contact and fair-ball assertions follow the reviewed MLB pitch-code distinctions;
- derived query-index facts may expose explicit current classifications only when traceable to the accepted authoritative evidence.

This aggregate record adds no ontology term or semantic rule. It authorizes the coordinated implementation of those accepted decisions across the existing detachable MLB-game lane and its downstream consumers.
