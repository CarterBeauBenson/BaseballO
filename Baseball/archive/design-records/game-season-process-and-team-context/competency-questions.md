# Competency questions

1. What kind of occurrent is a Baseball Game?
2. How are Baseball Acts, Baseball Physical Processes, and Baseball
   Institutional Processes related to a Baseball Game?
3. Does being prescribed by a Baseball Rule make the whole Baseball Game a
   Planned Act?
4. What kind of occurrent is a Baseball Season, and how are its Baseball Games
   and Baseball Season Phases related to it?
5. How does a Baseball Season Plan relate to a Baseball Season without making
   the Season an Information Content Entity or a Planned Act?
6. How is a Person's team context represented through an existing Occupation
   Role and Baseball Team?
7. How is the interval during which that role persists represented without a
   second roster-membership role?

Negative tests:

- a process is not a Planned Act merely because a Plan or Rule prescribes it;
- a Baseball Act is an occurrent part of a Baseball Game, not the whole game;
- a Baseball Season Plan is not the Baseball Season it prescribes; and
- team context is not an intrinsic quality or permanent identifier of a
  Person.
