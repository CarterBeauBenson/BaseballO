# Source evidence

The MLB transaction vocabulary distinguishes Released, Declared Free Agency,
and Retired. The codes are not synonyms and do not by themselves establish the
same world Process.

The reviewed institutional model proposes Baseball Player Release Act,
Free Agency Declaration Act, Retirement Declaration Act, and their Directive
outputs. Existing Gain of Role, Loss of Role, Player Role, and the proposed
Major League Free Agent Role supply independently grounded consequences.

Mapping remains code- and evidence-specific: release requires Team agency and
the exact team Role; a free-agency declaration requires Person agency; an
automatic free-agency rule consequence is not a declaration; retirement must
identify the exact active Role it ends. No raw response is retained.
