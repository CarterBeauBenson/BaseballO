# Competency questions

| ID | Question | Candidate answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EXIT-01 | What grounds a Release Act? | Team agency, Person participation, release-decision output, and supported Loss of the exact team Player Role. |
| EXIT-02 | Does Release entail free agency? | No. Gain of Major League Free Agent Role requires independent rule and status evidence. |
| EXIT-03 | What does Declared Free Agency identify? | A person-agent declaration/election Act only when structured evidence supports it; automatic status acquisition is not that Act. |
| EXIT-04 | What does Retirement end? | Only the exact active Player Role or Major League Free Agent Role established by evidence. |

## Negative tests

- Release, free-agency declaration, and retirement are never collapsed.
- Retirement does not erase every baseball-related Role across a career.
- A descriptive transaction record is not the Act or its Directive output.
