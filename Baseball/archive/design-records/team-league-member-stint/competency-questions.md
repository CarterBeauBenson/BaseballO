# Competency questions

| ID | Question | Candidate answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TL-01 | How is membership represented? | An Organization Member Role inheres in the Team and has the League Organization as organizational context. |
| TL-02 | Is Division membership sufficient? | No. League and Division histories require independent assertions and evidence. |
| TL-03 | What is the identity and temporal pattern? | One uninterrupted stint per Role; Team and Role participate in Stasis between supported Gain and Loss Processes. |
| TL-04 | What evidence is admitted? | Season-scoped organization responses only after historical corpus tests, including known league changes, pass. |
| TL-05 | How are gaps handled? | First evidence may be left-censored; missing observations do not end membership; a supported League change ends the old Role and begins a new one. |

## Negative tests

- No Team-League aggregate parthood.
- No League membership inferred through Division nesting.
- No Stasis realization edge.
- No source row or season key used as a Role.
