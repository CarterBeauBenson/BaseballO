# Field and identity inventory

| Evidence | Classification | Candidate use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team and League IDs | identity/join-only | Identify Role bearer and organizational context. |
| season-scoped nesting | candidate historical evidence | Admit only after known-change corpus tests pass. |
| Division nesting | separate evidence family | Never entails League membership. |
| changed League context | transition evidence | Supports Loss of old Member Role and Gain of new Role at supported precision. |
| null/missing season | absence of evidence | Emit no placeholder and do not end membership. |

Member Role identity is Team + League + uninterrupted stint. A return after an
evidenced Loss creates a new Role. The organizations lane owns these claims.
