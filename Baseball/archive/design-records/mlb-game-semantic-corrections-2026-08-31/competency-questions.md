# Accepted competency-question answers

1. `buntAttemptStatus=true`, plus supported in-play bunt trajectories, grounds
   Bunt Act; a sacrifice result is not the act detector.
2. Genuine PA structure grounds PA existence. Completion and institutional
   result require separate evidence; administrative pseudo-plays are excluded.
3. Game end is the terminal baseball event producing the final state,
   corroborated by final/game-over state evidence—not universally a final PA.
4. Runner `movement.end` grounds institutional state, not physical touching.
5. Wild pitch and passed ball are scorer-recognized institutional events;
   neither independently proves a physical control-failure process.
6. An overturned review supports an original unresolved Decision, but not its
   opposite content unless explicitly supplied or separately approved.
7. Statcast pre-pitch state remains a later multi-source integration and may
   not cross into MLB-game RML or SHACL.
8. Measurement evaluation, source, method, and era remain explicit.
