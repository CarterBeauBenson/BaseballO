# September 9 metric gap answers

The user accepted answers 1–6, 8 and 9 in
[decision.json](decision.json). This record precedes their implementation.
The acceptance covers the stated analytical choices and evidence requirements;
it does not approve ontology vocabulary, graph identity, source completeness,
or semantic pins. Question 7 was answered “Say more” and remains unresolved.

For continuity, distinguish the person's overall trip around the bases from
each batter-attributed or independent contribution within that trip. A steal
from first to second and a later single advancing second to third can belong
to one personal trajectory while remaining separate contribution episodes.
The single gets only second-to-third credit. Replacement ends the first
person's trajectory and starts another person's trajectory.

The proposed evidence standard requires an independently reconciled complete
history, with supported endpoints, movements, outcomes, substitutions and
inning boundaries. Source-row adjacency, matching IDs, a missing movement
row, or a previous safe outcome does not establish that completeness. The
method of proving that history and its RDF identity remain under review; no
continuity reducer or new graph assertion is admitted by this explanation.
