# Source evidence

The accepted people review observed `pitchHand` codes `L` and `R` and marked
the field as an authoritative duplicate of `gameData.players.*`. The source
field names a pitching context, but the ontologist selected the more general
baseball Throwing Side Disposition borne by the Person: every player has a
throwing side, and its realization is not limited to Pitch Acts.

Accepted BaseballO vocabulary supplies Person and Throw Act. BFO supplies
Disposition, inherence, participation, and realization. Generic Nominal
Measurement ICE and Reference System patterns carry the provider
classification without replacing the world-side disposition.

The source record does not identify a Hand individual, a particular Throw Act,
or a complete history. No raw API bytes were retained for this proposal.
