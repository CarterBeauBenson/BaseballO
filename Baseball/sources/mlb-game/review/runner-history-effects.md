# Accounted effects within accepted personal histories

This implements the existing E1/C1 complete-history criterion, Q4 actual
batter separation, and the accepted policy that ordinary scoring does not
require an original review call. It creates no term, predicate, identity
policy or new approval. The existing C1 operational review owns the scoped
context-builder pin; the global semantic freeze remains unratified.

For pitch reviews, the final source must explicitly report completed review,
consistent outcome and mechanism, a compatible final Ball/Strike call, and
the corresponding exact count increment. A terminal review's narrative and
structured disposition must agree. Unknown, pending, conflicting and
field-play reviews do not enter this path. This accounts for the operative
effect on a runner history and a normative award; it does not reconstruct an
original call, emit additional review RDF, or admit review eligibility.

An explicitly reconciled PH replacement with no runner effect can preserve
the same runners' histories. A PR replacement cannot use that exception.
Intermediate movement episodes retain their already accepted episode
identities. They need no additional lifetime anchor: the accepted personal
history identity remains tied to positively evidenced entry and termination.
Missing entry or termination anchors continue to withhold the history.

The original game 566279 now reconciles all half-innings, 31 personal histories
and all 13 scoring histories. Game 824087 reconciles 21 personal histories,
37 episode memberships and all seven scoring histories. Its unsupported
non-scoring halves remain explicitly withheld; complete scoring-run admission
does not claim complete offensive, baserunning or review populations.

The source corpus and RML templates are unchanged. Existing source SHACL
checks the resulting accepted C1 graph. The counted-run profile independently
checks the final run/roster census before either run player producer can serve
a complete selected range. Focused counterexamples cover unknown review
effects, conflicting counts, PR replacement and missing lifetime anchors.
