## Additional engineering defects fixed

1. **Input retirement race.** The award worker selected game 822748's input
   before acquiring the game lock. The history worker then promoted and retired
   that input at `2026-10-02T06:37:04.979469Z`; the award attempt failed two
   seconds later. Award, runner and compound repair wrappers now use the same
   existing resource lease as foul, defensive and history repairs, including
   their selection step. Their schedules and source-game locks remain in place.
2. **Incorrect retry identity.** The award worker treated an old per-game
   success or two failures as final even when the implementation or input bytes
   differed. Completion and retry limits now bind both the implementation and
   source hash. Terminal records retain the complete source witness.
3. **Transient inventory disappearance.** An input removed after directory
   enumeration could abort the whole award scan. The scan now skips that
   vanished entry and continues; changed bytes still receive a distinct hash.

The recorded 822748 attempt is resumed through the existing award worker and

Focused regressions cover exact-source retry limits, a retired inventory entry,
